\chapter{Sources and Verification}
\label{app:sources}

This appendix records how the evidence in the dossier was gathered, and
which figures could not be confirmed against a primary source.

\section{How the evidence was gathered}

\paragraph{Source papers}
The four supplied PDFs were converted to text and read in full, including
their reference lists. Their tables were lost in conversion, so every
results table was read again from rendered images of the original pages.
Disagreements between a paper's text and its tables are listed in
Table~\ref{tab:inconsistencies}.

\paragraph{Literature survey}
Searches covered arXiv, conference proceedings (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS,
ICLR, ICML, AAAI, ACM MM), journal sites and official repositories up to
2~October~2026. Work from 2026 was given priority. Numbers were taken from
primary pages, meaning arXiv abstracts or full text, official GitHub
repositories and official documentation, wherever these could be reached.

\paragraph{Datasets and tools}
Dataset statistics and licences were taken from official dataset pages and
repositories. Software versions were taken from the Python Package Index on
the dates stated.

\section{Figures that could not be confirmed}

The following items are marked with a dagger (\unv) in the text. They came
from secondary sources or from search snippets. Each must be checked against
the primary source before it is quoted in a manuscript.

\begin{itemize}
  \item UAVDet's reported parameter counts of 3.74M and 3.40M. They are
        printed in the paper but are inconsistent with its other tables.
  \item CFMW's LLVIP and SWVI mAP values, which differ between the tables
        and the text of the paper.
  \item The COCO AP of DINOv3 7B with Plain-DETR (65.6).
  \item EFSI-DETR's VisDrone results (33.1 AP, 24.8 AP\textsubscript{S}).
  \item The DanceTrack HOTA of MOTRv2, OC-SORT and ByteTrack, and the
        SportsMOT HOTA of the SAM~3 mask-propagation method.
  \item The SODA size thresholds.
  \item The licence of DroneVehicle.
  \item Kaggle's weekly GPU quota.
  \item The postponement of the EU AI Act's high-risk obligations to
        December 2027.
  \item The commencement dates of India's DPDP Rules.
  \item The journal impact factors in Table~\ref{tab:journals}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Statements of absence}

Several conclusions in the dossier rest on the \emph{absence} of prior work.
Examples are the absence of a causal, constant-memory state-space detector
for visible--thermal streams, of a benchmark protocol for temporal sensor
failure, and of a native TensorRT operator for two-dimensional selective
scanning. These statements mean only that the searches did not find such
work. The field publishes rapidly, and two connected literature services
could not be queried because they required authorisation. The searches must
therefore be repeated before submission, as Section~\ref{sec:submission}
requires.
