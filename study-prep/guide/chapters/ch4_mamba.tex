\chapter{Stage three: state-space models and Mamba}

\section{Why it matters}

This is the stage that standard computer-vision courses do not cover, and it is the heart of \method{}. The
fusion block interleaves visible and thermal features into one sequence and runs a selective state-space scan
over it. The project's central idea is a single multiplication inside that scan, so the scan itself has to be
understood exactly.

\section{The one equation to own}

A selective state-space model keeps a small hidden state $h$ per channel and walks along the sequence:
\[
  h_k = \exp(\Delta_k A)\, h_{k-1} + \Delta_k B_k x_k, \qquad y_k = C_k h_k + D x_k .
\]
$A$ is a learned negative diagonal, so $\exp(\Delta_k A)$ is a number between zero and one: how much of the past
is kept. $\Delta_k$, $B_k$ and $C_k$ are computed from the input itself, which is what makes the scan
\emph{selective}~\cite{gu2023mamba}. When $\Delta_k \to 0$ the state is kept and the input ignored; when
$\Delta_k$ is large the past is forgotten. \method{} multiplies $\Delta_k$ by the reliability of the camera that
produced token $k$, so a token from an unreliable camera cannot write into the state.

\section{What to learn}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Continuous state-space models and their discretisation by zero-order hold.
  \item The S4 view~\cite{gu2022s4}: the same model as a recurrence and as a long convolution.
  \item Selectivity in Mamba~\cite{gu2023mamba}, the parallel scan, and why it is linear in sequence length.
  \item Mamba-2 and the link between state-space models and linear attention~\cite{dao2024mamba2}.
  \item Applying a one-dimensional scan to an image: scan orders and directions~\cite{zhu2024vim,liu2024vmamba}.
  \item The sceptical view~\cite{yu2025mambaout}: when a gated convolution does as well, and why the project
        includes exactly that control.
  \item Hybrids and detectors~\cite{hatamizadeh2025mambavision,wang2025mambayolo}.
\end{itemize}
