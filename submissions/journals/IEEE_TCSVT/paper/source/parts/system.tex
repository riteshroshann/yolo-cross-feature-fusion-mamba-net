\section{The Full System}
\label{sec:system}
The detector is one stage of a pipeline that runs from cameras to counts. Two paired cameras feed the live runtime of Section~\ref{sec:live}, whose monitor converts stream health into sensor flags. CFFM-Net with its Temporal State Memory produces detections, ByteTrack turns them into tracks, and records of latency, health and track state feed the analysis boards. A separate layer takes detections from several calibrated cameras and produces anonymous ground-plane tracks and aggregate analytics.

\subsection{Multi-camera layer}
The multi-camera layer uses a plain RGB YOLO26-m detector, not CFFM-Net. WILDTRACK~\cite{chavdarova2018wildtrack} has \cfgCamViews{} synchronised, calibrated visible cameras and no thermal channel, so the layer demonstrates the system's last stage, from boxes to analytics, and provides no evidence about fusion. We evaluate the same layer with the detector zero-shot from COCO weights and fine-tuned on the training frames, which are exported as per-view person boxes. Frames are sampled every \cfgWtStep{} frames, and the frames from index \cfgWtTestFrom{} on are held out of the export.

\paragraph{From boxes to the ground plane}
Camera calibration gives a homography between the ground plane, in centimetres, and each image. The bottom centre of each person box is mapped to the ground, and points that fall outside the monitored area or whose back-projected ray misses the ground in front of the camera are discarded. Points from different cameras are merged by visibility-normalised voting. A candidate position is kept only if enough of the cameras that could see a person standing there did detect one near it, which avoids penalising people who stand in a blind spot of most cameras. The merging radius is \camFusionFineTunedRadius{}\,cm for both detectors, the required fraction of visible cameras is \camFusionFineTunedMinRatio{}, and the score threshold is \camFusionZeroShotMinScore{} for the zero-shot detector and \camFusionFineTunedMinScore{} for the fine-tuned one.

\paragraph{Tracking and analytics}
Fused positions feed a constant-velocity tracker on the ground plane with Hungarian association, a gate of \cfgTrackGate{}\,cm, a track kept through \cfgTrackMiss{} missed frames, and confirmation after \cfgTrackHits{} hits. Identifiers are ephemeral and anonymous. The analytics are aggregate: counts per zone and in total, crossings of a line in each direction, dwell time per zone, and an occupancy map on a grid of \cfgGroundCell{}\,cm cells. As a privacy measure, the head region of every person box is blurred before any image is stored or displayed.

\subsection{Results}
Detection, tracking and counting are reported in full in Supplementary Table~S7. Fine-tuning lifts the detector from a zero-shot MODA of \camDetZeroShotModa{}\,\% to \camDetFineTunedModa{}\,\%. On the ground-plane tracks the system reaches MOTA \camTrackMota{}\,\% and IDF1 \camTrackIdfOne{}\,\% with \camTrackSwitches{} identity switches, and the mean absolute error of the total count is \camCountMae{} people, a relative error of \camCountRelErr{}\,\%. These results describe the multi-camera layer on RGB input. Tracking and counting run on the fine-tuned YOLO26-m detections. They say nothing about whether visible-thermal fusion improves analytics, which would require paired multi-camera thermal data that WILDTRACK lacks.
